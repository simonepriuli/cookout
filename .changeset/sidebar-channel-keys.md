Web UI: the sidebar's links (Inbox, People, the channels, your profile and Settings) are one Tab stop, with ↑ and ↓ moving between them and Enter opening one. Alt+↓ and Alt+↑ open the next and previous channel in the sidebar's order from anywhere, the message box included, and Alt+Shift+↓ and Alt+Shift+↑ the next and previous channel with unread messages, saying "No unread channels" when none is left. On a focused channel row, E marks it all read and S subscribes or unsubscribes, each read out to screen readers; the channel menu shows both keys.
